Our Story

At Lakeview Coffee Roastery, we believe every cup tells a story. Since our founding, we've been dedicated to sourcing the finest beans from around the world and roasting them to perfection right here in Oswego. Our passion for quality and community drives everything we do, from the careful selection of our beans to the warm atmosphere we create for our guests.
